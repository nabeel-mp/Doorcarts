How It Works

From Order to Delivered

The complete lifecycle — placing an order, warehouse pick & pack, delivery assignment, and payment confirmation — handled seamlessly inside Doorcarts.

1

Place Order

Customer or trade pro selects materials, chooses delivery date, and pays via UPI, card, or credit account.

2

Branch Confirms

Branch ERP receives order, checks stock, confirms pricing, and generates a pick list for the warehouse team.

3

Pack & Assign

Order is packed, photographed for proof, and assigned to the nearest available delivery partner via the BuildShip app.

4

Deliver & Confirm

Delivery boy navigates to site, collects OTP from customer, and marks delivered. Payment settles automatically.

5

Sync & Report

All data syncs to branch ERP and cloud dashboard. Ledger updates, stock reduces, commission credits — all automatic.
